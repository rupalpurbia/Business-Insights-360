# Business Insights

## Dashboard Context

This Power BI dashboard analyses business performance across Finance, Sales, Marketing, Supply Chain and Executive views. The analysis is based on 2022 Estimated performance, benchmarked against last year and selected business metrics.

Amounts are shown in million £ unless stated otherwise.

---

## 1. Finance View

### Key Performance Summary

- Net Sales reached £3.74bn, compared with a benchmark of £823.85M, showing a strong increase of 353.50%.
- Gross Margin % improved to 38.08%, compared with the benchmark margin of 36.49%.
- Despite strong revenue and margin performance, Net Profit % remained negative at -13.98%, compared with the benchmark of -6.63%.
- Net Profit stood at -£522.42M, indicating that the business is generating revenue but still facing profitability pressure.

### Profit & Loss Insights

- Gross Sales were £7,370.14M, reducing to Net Sales of £3,736.17M after invoice and post-invoice deductions.
- Gross Margin was £1,422.88M, supported by Total COGS of £2,313.29M.
- Operational Expense was -£1,945.30M, which had the biggest impact on final profitability.
- The main business issue is not revenue generation, but converting revenue and gross margin into positive net profit.

### Regional and Segment Insights

- APAC generated the highest net sales at £1,924M, followed by NA at £1,022M and EU at £775M.
- NA showed the strongest growth change at 474.40%, followed by LATAM at 368.40% and APAC at 335.27%.
- Notebook was the highest revenue segment at £1,580M, followed by Peripherals at £898M and Desktop at £711M.
- Desktop showed the highest segment growth change at 1,431.55%, while Networking showed a negative change of -14.89%.

### Business Takeaway

The business has achieved strong revenue growth and improved gross margin, but high operational expenses are preventing profitability. Future focus should be on cost control, margin protection and improving net profit conversion.

## 2. Sales View

### Customer Performance Insights

- Amazon was the highest revenue-generating customer with £496.88M in net sales and £182.77M gross margin.
- AtliQ Exclusive generated £361.12M in net sales with a strong gross margin of 46.01%.
- AtliQ e Store contributed £304.10M in net sales and £112.15M gross margin.
- Neptune and Walmart showed strong gross margin percentages of 46.70% and 45.66%, despite lower net sales compared with Amazon.
- Sage and Leader had lower margin percentages at 31.53% and 30.70%, indicating weaker profitability efficiency.

### Product Performance Insights

- Notebook was the strongest product segment, generating £1,580.43M in net sales and £600.96M gross margin.
- Peripherals generated £897.54M in net sales and £341.22M gross margin.
- Desktop generated £711.08M in net sales and £272.39M gross margin.
- Networking had the smallest revenue contribution at £38.43M, but had the highest gross margin percentage at 38.45%.
- Overall gross margin across product segments was 38.08%.

### Revenue Deduction Insights

- Net Sales contributed 50.69% of the revenue split.
- Total Post Invoice Deduction represented 25.87%.
- Pre Invoice Deduction represented 23.43%.
- The split shows that deductions are a major factor between gross sales and realised net sales.

### Business Takeaway

Sales performance is driven mainly by a few large customers and high-volume product segments such as Notebook, Peripherals and Desktop. However, some lower-revenue customers and smaller segments show stronger margin efficiency, which could support more profitable growth if scaled carefully.

## 3. Marketing View

### Product Profitability Insights

- Notebook generated the highest net sales at £1,580.43M and the highest gross margin at £600.96M.
- Peripherals generated £897.54M in net sales and £341.22M gross margin.
- Desktop generated £711.08M in net sales and £272.39M gross margin.
- All product segments showed negative net profit, with Notebook recording the largest loss at -£222.16M.
- Networking had the smallest revenue contribution at £38.43M, but the highest gross margin percentage at 38.45%.

### Market Performance Insights

- APAC was the largest market by net sales at £1,923.77M and gross margin of £690.21M.
- NA generated £1,022.09M in net sales and delivered the strongest gross margin percentage at 44.97%.
- EU generated £775.48M in net sales, with a gross margin percentage of 34.53%.
- LATAM was the smallest market at £14.82M in net sales but had the lowest net profit loss at -£0.44M.
- APAC had the largest net profit loss at -£281.16M, showing that high revenue does not automatically translate into profitability.

### Margin and Cost Insights

- Gross Margin was £1,422.88M, representing 38.08% of sales.
- Total COGS was £2,313M, representing 61.92%.
- Operational Expense was approximately £1.9bn, leading to a final net profit loss of approximately £0.5bn.

### Business Takeaway

The marketing view shows that revenue volume is strong, especially in APAC and Notebook, but profitability remains weak across products and markets. The business needs to focus on high-margin markets, control operating expenses and improve return from high-volume segments.

## 4. Supply Chain View

### Forecast Accuracy Summary

- Forecast Accuracy was 81.17%, compared with 80.21% last year.
- Absolute Error improved from 9.78M last year to 6.90M, showing a 29.46% reduction.
- Net Error was -3.47M compared with -0.75M last year, showing a wider negative forecast variance.
- Overall forecast accuracy has improved slightly, but net error still needs attention.

### Customer-Level Supply Chain Insights

- AtliQ e Store had strong forecast accuracy at 74.22%, slightly below last year’s 74.59%.
- Amazon recorded 73.79% forecast accuracy, compared with 74.54% last year.
- AtliQ Exclusive recorded 70.35% forecast accuracy, compared with 71.69% last year.
- Acclaimed Stores improved from 50.69% last year to 57.74%.
- All-Out improved significantly from 29.09% last year to 43.96%.
- Several customers are still marked as OOS risk, showing potential stock availability or demand planning pressure.

### Product-Level Supply Chain Insights

- Networking had the highest forecast accuracy at 93.06%.
- Desktop and Accessories also performed strongly, with forecast accuracy of 87.53% and 87.42%.
- Notebook achieved 87.24% forecast accuracy.
- Peripherals had the weakest forecast accuracy at 68.17%, with a net error of -3.20M and net error percentage of -31.83%.
- Storage also showed weaker performance with 71.50% forecast accuracy and net error percentage of -25.61%.

### Business Takeaway

Forecast accuracy improved overall, mainly supported by stronger performance in Networking, Desktop, Accessories and Notebook. However, Peripherals and Storage are clear pressure areas and should be prioritised for demand planning, inventory review and forecast correction.

## 5. Executive View

### Overall Executive Summary

- Net Sales reached £3.74bn, significantly higher than the benchmark of £823.85M.
- Gross Margin % was 38.08%, above the benchmark of 36.49%.
- Net Profit % remained negative at -13.98%, showing that profitability is still the main business concern.
- Forecast Accuracy was 81.17%, slightly better than last year’s 80.21%.

### Revenue Split Insights

- PC division contributed £2.3bn in revenue.
- P&A division contributed £1.4bn in revenue.
- Retailer channel contributed £2.7bn, making it the dominant channel in the revenue mix.

### Sub-Region Performance Insights

- NA generated £1,022.09M in net sales with the highest gross margin percentage of 44.97%.
- India generated £945.34M in net sales but had the weakest net profit percentage at -22.99%.
- SE generated £317.78M in net sales and had the lowest net profit loss percentage at -4.00%.
- ANZ generated £189.78M in net sales with a strong gross margin percentage of 43.50%.
- Overall net profit percentage across sub-regions was -13.98%.

### Top Customers and Products

- Amazon had the highest revenue contribution among top customers, with 13.3% revenue contribution and 36.78% gross margin.
- AtliQ Exclusive had 9.7% revenue contribution and the strongest gross margin among listed customers at 46.01%.
- AtliQ e Store contributed 8.1% revenue with 36.88% gross margin.
- The top listed products together contributed 23.2% revenue contribution, with an average gross margin of 38.06%.

### Market Share Insights

- AtliQ’s PC market share was 5.9% in 2022 Est.
- Competitors showed stronger market share positions, with Dale at 22.3%, Innovo at 9.9%, BP at 7.9% and Pacer at 7.6%.
- AtliQ’s market share appears lower than key competitors, indicating a need for stronger competitive positioning in the PC market.

### Business Takeaway

The executive view shows a business with strong revenue growth, stable gross margin and improving forecast accuracy. However, profitability and competitive market share remain the key strategic challenges. The main focus should be on reducing operating cost pressure, improving net profit and strengthening AtliQ’s position in the PC market.
